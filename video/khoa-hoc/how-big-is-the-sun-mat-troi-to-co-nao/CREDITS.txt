How big is the Sun? — a Dark Science Data film for Andrew Classes
All pictures are drawn by code (film.js). No generated images, no photos, no third-party artwork.

Narration
  Teacher Andrew's own cloned voice ("Andrew 26/10 · kể chuyện"), recorded locally by myStudio.
Facts
  NASA, Sun Fact Sheet (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/sunfact.html) and NASA Science "Sun: Facts":
  diameter about 1.39 million km (109 Earths), volume about 1.3 million Earths, light time about 8 min 20 s,
  99.8 % of the Solar System's mass, a medium-size (G-type) star. Star row sizes are not to scale.
