How far can an owl turn its head? — Colored-Pencil Science Book (Tranh chì màu khoa học), Andrew Classes

Pictures
  Painted for this film by a local image model (Animagine, run on the teacher's own computer) from original prompts;
  short real-motion clips made locally from those pictures with an image-to-video model (Wan 2.2). No third-party artwork.
  Paper, pencil lines, the eye diagram, the neck-bone beads, labels and transitions are drawn in code.
Narration
  Teacher Andrew's own cloned voice, generated locally in myStudio.
Lettering
  Patrick Hand — Patrick Wagesreiter, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (fonts/OFL-patrickhand.txt).
  Dancing Script — Impallari Type, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Music samples (CC0 1.0)
  Versilian Community Sample Library (Versilian Studios): harp, marimba, glockenspiel, vibraphone (bowed), ocarina.
Score, foley and ambience
  Original, composed and synthesized in music.py and mix.py.
Tools
  Lemo-Opuscar library (MIT): render, mux, subtitles; faster-whisper (MIT) for the speech check.
